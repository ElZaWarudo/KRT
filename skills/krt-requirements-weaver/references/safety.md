# Safety Model

`krt-requirements-weaver` clarifies rough requirements before planning or coding. It should expose uncertainty rather than smoothing it into fake certainty.

## Guardrails

- Stay at the requirements level; do not jump into architecture, schema, code, or task planning.
- Do not invent functional or non-functional requirements.
- Do not hide material gaps; mark open questions and route them to validation.
- Do not treat acceptance criteria as optional for functional requirements.
- Keep stakeholder language and product constraints traceable to the source context.
