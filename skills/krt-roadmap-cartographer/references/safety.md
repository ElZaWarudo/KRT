# Safety Model

`krt-roadmap-cartographer` generates one roadmap or readiness report from existing context. It should map reality, not manufacture confidence.

## Guardrails

- Do not generate a draft roadmap when context is insufficient.
- Do not create auxiliary primary artifacts unless the workflow explicitly requires them.
- Do not convert uncertainty into scope; record uncertainty as blockers, assumptions, or validation needs.
- Keep recommendations traceable to source context.
- Do not imply commitment to schedule, staffing, or delivery dates without evidence.
